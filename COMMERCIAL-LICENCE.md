The full text is in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) at the root of this repository. That

## A separate commercial licence is planned

A **separate, proprietary commercial licence** covering the same source code is
planned. It will be granted by the copyright holder as a second, independent
grant — not as a surcharge on the AGPL grant, and not as a "paid tier" of it.

**Those terms do not exist yet.** They are being finalised and have not been
written, reviewed or signed. Nothing in this repository grants, describes, prices
or promises a commercial licence. Do not treat any statement here as an offer.

When the commercial licence is issued it will be published as a separate document
in this repository, linked from this file and from the README.

## Why a second grant is needed

AGPL-3.0-only is the open-source tier. A business that must keep its own source
closed cannot obtain that by paying under the AGPL grant: section 5(c) requires
the whole work to be licensed under AGPL to anyone it conveys a copy to, and
section 10 forbids charging for the exercise of rights the licence already grants
freely. The only lawful route to a closed-source deployment is a separate
copyright licence from the holder. That document does not exist yet.

## Where the reasoning lives

The model behind this decision, including the open questions, is recorded
internally in `LICENCE_MODEL.md` in the NumFast dev-env repository, which is
private and is not a published source of licence terms. Three specific points in it — the
network-use boundary, the "mere aggregation" boundary, and the sole-copyright-holder
and patent position — require a qualified lawyer and are unresolved.

> "You may charge any price for warranty or support or indemnity for damages you
> may have caused to the recipients of the Program… and You may offer support or
> warranty protection for a fee." — AGPL-3.0 section 4

Selling support, consultancy or indemnity alongside the AGPL grant requires no
further paperwork. It does not require the commercial licence to exist.
